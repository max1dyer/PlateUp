1. We are going to stop hiding our judgements or opinions on matters of the project and the direction we should take.
2. We are not going to judge teammates on their lack of knowledge in specific areas of the project.
3. Stop assuming that other people will eventually notice or fix work that has been incomplete.

## Explicit rules for blocker escalations

1. If a teammate is unable to make progress on a specific feature for 48 hours, that teammate should seek help from the rest of the team in order to progress towards completion.
2. Members must move the Jira ticket to "Blocked" and assign a comment describing their exact issue and what they have tried thus far.
3. Members must directly communicate with another team member that they think will have the best insight in how to overcome the blocker.
